FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the ebooks really licensed for AI training?

Yes. Grovix holds the rights to license its catalog for AI and machine-learning use. Every agreement states the permitted uses, such as training, fine-tuning and evaluation.

What does ISBN certification mean for my dataset?

Every title has a registered ISBN, a globally unique identifier. This makes each work traceable, so you can document data provenance for compliance, audits and model cards.

Which formats do you deliver?

Plain text, JSONL with metadata, EPUB and PDF. We can also deliver pre-chunked or custom-tokenised files.

Can I get a sample before buying?

Yes. Request a sample using the form below and we'll send a representative set with full metadata, usually within 48 hours.

Do you offer exclusive licenses?

Exclusive and semi-exclusive licenses are available on Enterprise agreements for selected collections.
