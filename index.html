Questions

Asked often.

How is AIHive related to Grovix Ventures?

AIHive is a brand of Grovix Ventures Pvt Ltd. Grovix Ebooks licenses content for AI training, and AIHive gives Indian businesses an AI sales platform. For AIHive accounts and support, visit getaihive.in.

Are the ebooks licensed for AI training?

Yes. Each agreement states the permitted uses, such as pre-training, fine-tuning and evaluation, along with scope and duration.

Which formats do you deliver?

Plain text, JSONL with metadata, EPUB and PDF. Pre-chunked files can be arranged on request.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Send us your brief and we'll share a representative sample with full metadata before you commit.

How is pricing decided?

Every licence is custom. We quote based on the subjects, volume, permitted use, exclusivity and delivery you need. Send us your brief for a quote.

Do you offer exclusive licences?

Yes. Exclusive and semi-exclusive terms are available for selected collections, agreed as part of your custom licence.
